Extractive vs Abstractive Summarization Insight

Extractive (TextRank): Directly scores, ranks, and extracts unaltered verbatim sentences from the source document.
Abstractive (BART / T5): Uses Seq2Seq neural attention to paraphrase, synthesize, and generate novel sentences like a human reader.

🌿 TextRank (Extractive)
Extracts top-ranked sentences directly from source without changing vocabulary. Run "Compare All Models" to inspect.
🧠 BART / T5 (Abstractive)
